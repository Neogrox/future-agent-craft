# AI Forge Labs

Build a modern, high-converting website for [AGENCY NAME], an AI agency that designs 

and builds AI agents, workflow automation, and offers AI strategy consulting for 

businesses. Position us as experts who go beyond "just automation" — we build custom 

AI agents, integrate them into existing business systems, and advise leadership on 

AI adoption strategy.

BRAND & TONE

- Vibe: bold, futuristic, confident — not another generic SaaS template. Think dark, 

  premium, slightly cinematic (subtle gradients, glassmorphism or grain texture, 

  glowing accents) rather than flat corporate blue-and-white.

- Primary color: [your color/palette, e.g. deep navy/black + electric violet/teal accent]

- Typography: a strong modern display font for headings (e.g. Space Grotesk, Clash 

  Display, or similar), clean sans-serif for body text

- Motion: smooth scroll-triggered animations, subtle hover states, a hero section 

  with an animated/interactive element (e.g. a flowing particle network, animated 

  agent workflow diagram, or live-typing terminal effect) that visually signals "AI"

STRUCTURE / SECTIONS

1. Hero — bold headline + subheadline, primary CTA ("Book a Strategy Call"), 

   animated visual

2. Trust bar — logos/stats placeholder (clients served, agents deployed, hours saved)

3. Services — three pillars, each with icon, short description, and a "learn more" 

   link: (a) AI Agents & Automation (custom agent builds, workflow automation, 

   integrations), (b) AI Consulting & Strategy (audits, roadmaps, implementation 

   advisory), (c) Managed AI Services / ongoing support

4. How we work — a simple 3–4 step process (Discover → Design → Build → Deploy & 

   Support), shown as a horizontal timeline or numbered cards

5. Case studies / results — 2–3 placeholder project cards with problem → solution → 

   outcome metrics

6. Why us — differentiators (not a template shop, full-stack from strategy to 

   deployment, industry-specific agents, etc.)

7. Testimonials — carousel or grid, placeholder quotes

8. Pricing or "packages" teaser (optional — can be "custom, book a call" instead of 

   fixed pricing)

9. FAQ — accordion, 5–6 common questions about AI agents/automation/consulting

10. Final CTA — strong closing section with contact form / calendar booking embed

11. Footer — nav, socials, contact info, newsletter signup

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Fully responsive (mobile-first), fast-loading, accessible (proper contrast, alt text)

- Smooth scroll and section-reveal animations (Framer Motion / GSAP style)

- Interactive elements should feel alive but not gimmicky — no fake spinning loaders 

  just for show

- Contact form should be functional (connect to email or a form service)

- SEO basics: proper meta tags, title, OG image placeholder, semantic HTML
